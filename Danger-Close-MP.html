Danger Close

You are a civilian in a district that SPECTRE 2-1 has orders to level. Stay out of the open, slip through lit doors to hide, and get out before the gunship brings your building down. When the beacon lights, reach the pad.

3D graphics are not available in this browser, so the game cannot start.

WASD / left stickMove. Hold Shift to run (loud). Mouse / drag right sideLook around. Click the view to capture the mouse. Lit doorWalk into it to hide. Walk into the green exit to leave. SpaceJump. Clears cars, benches and hydrants, and you can land on top of them. Jumping is noisy. Fire stairsMid-rise blocks have outdoor maintenance stairs on one side. Walk up them to the roof. RampsWalk up the ramps inside a building to reach its upper floors. The top ramp leads out to the roof, which is open sky: jump between roofs, but a long fall hurts. EAt a street hatch: go down into the sewer. At a ladder: climb out. Hold E to dig out of a sealed hatch. Q · P · MThrow smoke, pause, mute. Z X C VSquad callouts (squad play only).

Running in the open raises your signature. Above the line, the gunship marks you. Inside a building or down a sewer you are hidden, but buildings and hatches can still be targeted.
